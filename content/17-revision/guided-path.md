Not everyone has the same amount of time or the same starting point. Pick the path that matches you and follow it in order. Each step links to the lessons to read.

## Path A: "My interview is tomorrow" (about 4 hours)

| Time | Do this |
|---|---|
| 30 min | Read [A Framework for Any Design Question](#/l/01-fundamentals/interview-framework) and [Back-of-the-Envelope Estimation](#/l/01-fundamentals/back-of-the-envelope). Practise one estimate aloud. |
| 45 min | Skim [50 HLD Concepts](#/l/17-revision/hld-50); mark anything shaky and read only those lessons' key takeaways. |
| 60 min | Read three crisp case studies relevant to the company: e.g. [Instagram feed](#/l/14-crisp-case-studies/instagram-feed), [WhatsApp delivery](#/l/14-crisp-case-studies/whatsapp-delivery), [Amazon payments](#/l/14-crisp-case-studies/amazon-payments). |
| 45 min | Do one full design out loud against a 40-minute timer: [URL Shortener](#/l/10-hld-case-studies/url-shortener) or [Chat System](#/l/10-hld-case-studies/chat-system), then compare. |
| 30 min | [Rules of Thumb for Trade-offs](#/l/09-tradeoffs/heuristics) + [Interview Strategy & Mindset](#/l/17-revision/strategy). |
| 15 min | [Concept Flashcards](#/l/17-revision/flashcards). Then sleep. |

## Signs you're ready

- You can explain any concept from the [HLD 50](#/l/17-revision/hld-50) list in 30 seconds.
- You complete a timed design and score **14+** on the [lab checklist](#/l/13-design-it-yourself/how-to-use).
- When someone challenges a choice, you can name an alternative and the trade-off without hesitating.
- You can write a working LLD solution for a classic problem in 60–75 minutes.
